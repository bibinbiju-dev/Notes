# Cookies

It is used to store small amounts of data(around 4kb) in the browser. Unlike [[Local-Storage]] or [[Session-Storage]] cookies are **automatically sent to the server** with every HTTP request.

- it can be used used for:
  - **session management**
  - **user preferences**
  - **tracking and analytics**
- it is stored as single string of key-value pairs in document.cookie.
